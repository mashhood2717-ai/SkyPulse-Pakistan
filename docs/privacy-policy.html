Privacy Policy for SkyPulse Pakistan

Last Updated: May 10, 2026

SkyPulse Pakistan (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is developed by Mashhood Iqbal. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the SkyPulse Pakistan mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Location Data

  • We access your device’s GPS location to provide accurate, location-based weather forecasts.
  • Location data is processed on your device and sent only to public weather APIs (Open-Meteo, Aviation Weather METAR) to fetch weather for your current area.
  • Location data is NOT stored on our servers and is NOT shared with any third party for advertising or tracking.

Push Notification Token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

  • We collect an anonymous Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token to deliver weather alerts and storm notifications.
  • The token does not personally identify you.
  • Tokens are stored securely via Firebase (Google) with industry-standard encryption.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Location (Fine / Coarse): To deliver localized weather data.
  • Notifications: To send weather alerts (optional, user-controlled).
  • Internet: To fetch weather data from public APIs.
  • Vibrate: For alert notifications.
  • Receive Boot Completed: To restore alert subscriptions after device restart.

3. Third-Party Services

SkyPulse Pakistan uses the following services. Please review their respective privacy policies:

  • Open-Meteo — weather forecast data
  • Aviation Weather Center / NOAA — METAR aviation data
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging by Google — push notifications
  • Google Maps Platform — satellite imagery

4. Data Retention

  • We do not store any personal data on our servers.
  • FCM tokens are retained only as long as the app is installed on your device.
  • Cached weather data is stored locally on your device for offline access and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

5. Children’s Privacy

SkyPulse Pakistan is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

6. Your Rights

You can at any time:

  • Revoke location permission via Android Settings → Apps → SkyPulse → Permissions.
  • Disable notifications via Android Settings → Apps → SkyPulse → Notifications.
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove all local data and the FCM token.

7. Security

All network communication uses HTTPS. Firebase data is encrypted in transit and at rest using Google’s standard infrastructure security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
